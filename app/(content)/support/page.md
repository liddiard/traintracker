# ❤️ Support TrainTracker

TrainTracker completely free to use, and I plan to keep it that way. But it does cost money to keep running and develop. If you enjoy the site and can afford it, I’d really appreciate a donation to keep it chugging along and to invest into building [new features](https://github.com/liddiard/traintracker/issues).

Here’s what TrainTracker costs to run as of 2026:

- Web hosting: $7.00/mo.
- Domain name: $2.50/mo.

I’m very grateful if you choose to chip in. If there’s a feature from the list above that you’re passionate above, please include it in your message! I’ll read it and take it into account when planning what to add to TrainTracker next.
